A ternary that follows a ternary with an implicit call in its first branch now compiles. After `let v = c ? fn 1 : 2`, a later line such as `let w = foo d ? 3 : 4` failed with `Unexpected ')'`, because the compiler kept the first ternary open and ended the call to `foo` at the second colon. In templates, `<p> a ? <i> "m" : <s>` followed by `<p> b ? <a.x> : <i>` failed in the same way.
