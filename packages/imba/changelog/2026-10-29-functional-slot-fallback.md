A template method with fallback content in its slot, such as `def card` rendering `<div> <slot> "fallback"`, now shows the children it is called with from the first render. `<card()> <b> "given"` used to show `fallback` until the next render, because the method runs before the call site adds the children. The same delay happened whenever the children appeared or disappeared, for example behind an `if`. The call site now renders the method once more when its children change between the slot content and the fallback. Text-only children of a template call, as in `<card()> "label"`, now go into the slot as well. They used to be dropped when the method returned an element.
