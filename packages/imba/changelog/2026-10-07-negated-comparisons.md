Negate relational comparisons with `!` instead of swapping the operator. `unless`, `until` and `for … when` negate their condition, and for a comparison they swapped the operator: `unless a > 0` compiled to `if (a <= 0)`. That is not a negation when an operand is `NaN` or `undefined`, because every relational comparison is then false — so `throw new Error("x") unless a > 0` did not throw for `a = undefined`. These guards now compile to `!(a > 0)`. A chained comparison (`unless a < b < c`) is now negated as a whole; before, only its last comparison was negated. Equality operators still swap to their opposite (`unless a == b` compiles to `if (a != b)`).
