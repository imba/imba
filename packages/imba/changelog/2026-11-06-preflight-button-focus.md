Remove the `button:focus` outline from `imba/preflight.css`. The rule drew the browser focus ring on a button also after a mouse click, and it overrode an app's own `:focus` styles because its selector is more specific. Browsers now show the focus ring only for keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`), and the preflight keeps the Firefox `:-moz-focusring` rule. If an app needs a focus ring on every focused button, add a `button:focus` style to the app.
