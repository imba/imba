A member access or a call after `(rescue await x)` now applies to the awaited value. Before, `(rescue await load!).data` compiled to `await (async ()=>{ … })().data`, which reads `data` on the promise. So `(rescue await load!).data` and `(rescue await load!)..data` were always `undefined`, with no error, and `(rescue await load!).run!` threw a `TypeError`. The same was true for an index, for `(rescue await load!)!` and for `rescue(await load!).data`. `rescue await` now compiles to `(await (async ()=>{ … })())`.
