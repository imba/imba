A `for`, `while` or `switch` used as an expression, such as a call argument, now keeps `self` inside methods. When a method passed a loop to a call, as in `String(for x in list ...)`, the loop was wrapped in a plain `function`, where `this` is undefined, so reading a field such as `offset` in the loop body threw `Cannot read properties of undefined`. The same happened with postfix `for` and with `while` and `switch` expressions. These wrappers are now arrow functions.
